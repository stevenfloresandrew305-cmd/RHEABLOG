RHEA VILLANUEVA - PERSONAL BLOG
================================
1. Copy your 20 photos into the img/ folder as 1.jpg ... 20.jpg
2. Double-click index.html

No server, build step or internet connection is required
(an internet connection only loads the Gilda Display and Jost fonts;
the site falls back to Georgia / system fonts without it).

Editing captions
----------------
Open js/script.js and change the CAPTIONS list at the top.
Captions for photos 3-17 are the ones you supplied. Photos 1, 2 and 18-20
use neutral placeholders written without seeing the pictures; replace them
with captions that describe those photos.
